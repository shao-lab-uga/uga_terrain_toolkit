Every ground point within 25 cm of the chosen spot, from every scan, coloured by scan number. If the scans line up, the colours sit on top of each other in one thin band; a spread or a step between colours is a pose or mounting error. Lines show what the app measured.

What the files are

  • .xodr OpenDRIVE road network: reference line, elevation profile, banking (superelevation), driving lanes, curbs and sidewalks with their heights. Opens in CARLA, RoadRunner, esmini.
  • road_profile.csv one row per metre of road: elevation, grade, banking, cross slopes, widths, curb and sidewalk sizes, roughness, and the car's own pitch/roll for comparison.
  • ground.ply the merged ground surface (one point per 20 cm cell), coloured by surface type. Opens in CloudCompare, MeshLab.
  • rasters.zip elevation, slope, roughness, surface label and intensity as float TIFF tiles with .tfw world files (local metres, origin in summary.json).
  • trajectory.csv the car's final 3D pose for every lidar frame used.
  • summary.json map origin (lat/lon/alt), areas, roads and processing details.
